# Experiment EXP-025

Model-internal policy check: do retirees covered by a 0.6-replacement pension show lower mean stress than retirees without any pension over 2 years? Verifies the pension→runway→strain pathway inside the simulator; no real-world policy claim.

Metric: `stress.mean`. All values are model-internal aggregates over 3 seeded runs per arm.
