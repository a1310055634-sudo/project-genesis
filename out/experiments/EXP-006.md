# Experiment EXP-006

Model-internal mechanism check: does shifting the whole population's extraversion upward (+0.4 bias) produce a denser social network (more edges, higher mean degree) than an unbiased population over 2 years? Verifies the personality→network pathway inside the simulator; no real-world psychological claim.

Metric: `social_edges`. All values are model-internal aggregates over 3 seeded runs per arm.

| extraverted | 3 | 1513 ± 82.335554 | higher |

Seeds: 42, 43, 44 · population 150 · 2y per run.
