# Experiment EXP-001

Model-internal mechanism check: does a mid-run layoff shock (50% of employed at the start of year 1) produce higher mean stress than an economy that keeps full employment, measured after 2 simulated years? Verifies the shock→strain→stress pathway inside the simulator; no real-world causal claim.

Metric: `stress.mean`. All values are model-internal aggregates over 5 seeded runs per arm.

| arm | n | mean ± ci95 | direction vs baseline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| control | 5 | 0.511562 ± 0.013061 | baseline |
| shock | 5 | 0.539496 ± 0.013331 | higher |

Seeds: 42, 43, 44, 45, 46 · population 300 · 2y per run.
