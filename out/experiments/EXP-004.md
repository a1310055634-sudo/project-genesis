# Experiment EXP-004

Model-internal mechanism check: does doubling housing costs (housingCostMultiplier 2) raise mean stress and lower mean wellbeing relative to baseline rent over 2 years? Verifies the housing→strain→stress pathway inside the simulator; no real-world causal claim.

Metric: `stress.mean`. All values are model-internal aggregates over 3 seeded runs per arm.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| control | 3 | 0.489521 ± 0.015621 | baseline |
| high-rent | 3 | 0.516844 ± 0.016326 | higher |

Seeds: 42, 43, 44 · population 150 · 2y per run.
